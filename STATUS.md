# STATUS — read this first

Everything in the brief is built and was run. The work stopped once part-way (a usage
limit) and was resumed and finished afterwards.

## What was built
- **Rover** (`protos/MineRover.proto`): 4WD skid steer, every dimension a field, all sensors
  with the exact names from the brief, pan head with camera and headlamp, `useMeshes` switch.
- **Worlds**: `mine.wbt` plus `corridor`, `obstacles`, `pit_ahead`, `side_pit`, `gas_ahead`,
  `ramp`, all generated by `tools/build_worlds.py` in one style.
- **Autonomy** (`rover_core/`, pure Python): conditioning, attitude filter, fusion table,
  navigator, sweep sequencer, gas model, hazard log. All tuning in `config/rover.yaml`.
- **Controllers**: `rover_controller` (20 Hz autonomy, 5 ms safety loop) and
  `mine_supervisor` (gas, roof fall, metrics, scenarios, hazard label, chase camera, video).
- **Reports** per run: `hazard_log.csv`, `events.jsonl`, `sweeps/`, `metrics.json`, `summary.html`.

## Not verified, or weaker than it looks
- **Keyboard control was never exercised.** Nobody was at the keyboard. The key logic is
  unit-tested, but W A S D, Space, M, X, R, C, L, V and D in the live GUI are untested.
  Try them first.
- **`./run.sh` as a normal interactive session was not tried.** The GUI was only opened by
  the scenario runner (batch mode with a visible window).
- **The ramp in the mine's side bay is scenery.** The rover does not go there in AUTO; the
  ramp behaviour is only tested in the `ramp` world.
- **`useMeshes` was checked with a placeholder box STL only** (it loaded without errors).
- **The mine looks like a mine, but plainly.** Walls are rows of rock-textured columns; that
  shape is there for the sonar and reads a bit like a palisade.
- **Passing depends on those ribbed walls.** Webots' sonar cannot see a smooth wall at a
  glancing angle. With smooth walls the rover would scrape them.
- **The worlds are tidy and the runs are short.** 145 passes out of 145 shows the logic is
  consistent across noise and start-pose changes, not that it will survive a real tunnel.

## Decisions worth knowing (full list in DECISIONS.md)
- A / D steer only in MANUAL; otherwise A = AUTO and D = drop rock (Shift+A / Shift+D always).
- Textures are generated locally; Webots R2025a ships none on disk.
- Pipe and cable along the wall instead of a rail track.
- IR colour sensitivity is off; IMU noise, gyro bias, dead zone and motor mismatch are added
  in the controller.
- Navigator additions: AVOID needs 1 m of open room to exit and reverses after 100 degrees;
  one-sided pits get a sidestep; gentle centring between walls.
- Roof-fall additions: own-motion allowance, cancel if the rover turns, forward IR counts.
- A GateGuard hook on this machine blocks the first write of each new file, which slowed
  the build.
